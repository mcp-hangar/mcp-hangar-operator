**controller:** the Cilium-flavor MCPEgressPolicy backstop now denies port 53 to `world`. Before, a governed pod got answers from a DNS resolver on the internet even though the DNS allow rule names only the cluster resolver. That channel bypassed the egress allow-list, for example for DNS tunnelling. The Vanilla flavor was not affected. A policy with a CIDR upstream on port 53 outside the cluster is now denied on that port.

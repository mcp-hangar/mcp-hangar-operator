**api:** `MCPServerSpec` gains `initData`, the init-data document a confidential guest is launched with (ADR-031). On a container-mode server whose `spec.tls.peerIdentity.issuance` is `kbs-attested`, the CRD now requires `initData` naming `spec.tls.peerIdentity.keyRef`, a digest-pinned `spec.image`, no `env` taken from a Secret, only `emptyDir` volumes and `automountServiceAccountToken: false`. It refuses `initData` on any other server and on a discovery template. The operator does not yet carry the field into the pod.
